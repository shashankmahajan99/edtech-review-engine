DETAILED AUDIT FINDINGS & RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

1. NATIVE OPENXML WORD MARGIN COMMENTS INJECTION:
   - python-docx does not have native high-level methods for comments.
   - We must construct `word/comments.xml`, attach relationships in `word/_rels/document.xml.rels`,
     and inject `w:commentRangeStart`, `w:commentRangeEnd`, and `w:commentReference` in `document.xml`.
   - This produces identical margin balloons in MS Word, Google Docs, and LibreOffice.

2. TRIVIUM KNOWLEDGE BASE HYPERLINKS:
   - Trivium links their comments to `https://knowledge.thinkingstorm.com/...`.
   - We can configure institutional links to our own knowledge hub (`https://knowledge.apexreview.org/...`)
     or provide direct pedagogical guidance.

3. GPTZERO & TURNITIN DETECTION DEFENSE:
   - Trivium's actual comment:
     "INTRODUCTION The opening is too general to engage readers. What personal experience or realization drew you to healthcare, and how can you use it to create a more focused hook?"
   - Tested on GPTZero: 0% AI Probability.
   - Why? It is conversational, direct, ends in an open-ended Socratic question, and has no formal boilerplate.
   - Our `engine/anti_ai_humanizer.py` now replicates this exact Socratic cadence.
